Pricing questions

Straight answers

Anything else about pricing, email us and we reply within minutes.

Why is there no fixed price list?
A small ornament and a stone estate with a lantern build are very different jobs. A fixed list would overcharge simple models. Instead you get a fixed quote per order, usually within minutes.
Can the price change after I accept the quote?
No. The only exception is a change beyond the original order, for example adding a garage that was not in the photos. We always ask before doing that.
Are revisions included?
Yes. We revise until your customer approves, at no extra cost.
Do you charge extra for weekends?
No. 24 to 48 hours is our standard turnaround every day of the year, weekends and holidays included.
