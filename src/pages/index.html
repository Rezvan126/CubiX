All questions
Is the first model really free?
Yes, for every Etsy shop owner or team, and for any model, simple or complex. We model it, handle the revisions and deliver the final files. There is no obligation to continue.
Do you only model houses?
No. Houses are most of our portfolio, but we model anything you print: lanterns, keepsakes, landmarks, signs and custom pieces from photos or sketches.
How much does a model cost?
Usually $50 to $150, depending on size, detail, print method and extras. You get a fixed price before we start. Try the price estimator.
When do I pay?
After your customer approves the model and you have printed it. Through Upwork, Fiverr, Payoneer or directly.
