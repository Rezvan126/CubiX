Everything shops ask us

Is the first model really free?
Yes, for every Etsy shop owner or team, on any model, simple or complex. We model it, handle the revisions and deliver the final files. There is no obligation to continue.
Do you only model houses?
No. Houses are most of our portfolio, but we model anything you print: LED lanterns, keepsakes, ornaments, landmarks, signs and custom pieces from photos or sketches.
Can you turn a photo of a house into a 3D printable STL file?
Yes. That is our core service. Send a few photos of the house, or a Google Street View link, plus the size you print at. We build a watertight STL or 3MF file that is ready to slice, and send preview renders within 24 hours.
Do you work with 3D print farms and gift shops, not only Etsy sellers?
Yes. We model for Etsy shops, 3D print farms, gift and souvenir shops, makers and small brands. If you sell printed products and need custom 3D models, we can be your modeling team.
Can you model from a sketch, a drawing or Street View?
Yes. We work from customer photos, hand sketches, floor plans, elevation drawings, Google Street View and existing 3D files that need fixing for print.
How do I send an order?
Use the order page to upload photos and details, or email them to us. You can also order through Upwork or Fiverr.
What do you need for each order?
Photos, a sketch or drawings of the object (the front view is essential), the finished size, your printer and any special details such as pets, cars or a name plaque.
What if part of the object is not visible in the photos?
We match the style of the visible parts and point out what we assumed in the preview, so your customer can correct it.
How do I find you on Upwork or Fiverr?
Email us and we send you the direct link within minutes. You then hire us on the platform and pay through it after approval.
How much does a model cost?
Usually $50 to $150. The price depends on size, detail, print method and extras such as split parts or a lantern build. Try the estimator. You always get a fixed quote first.
When do I pay?
After your customer approves the model and you have printed it. There is no upfront payment and no deposit.
How can I pay?
Through Upwork, Fiverr, Payoneer, bank transfer or Wise. Regular shops can pay one monthly invoice.
Do you offer monthly rates?
Yes. Monthly packages for 10, 25 or 50+ models give you a lower rate, reserved capacity and one invoice. See monthly packages.
How fast is delivery?
Preview renders within 24 hours and final files within 48 hours at the latest.
Do you work weekends and holidays?
Yes. The 48 hour maximum applies every day of the year, including weekends, public holidays and Christmas week.
What if my customer is not happy?
We revise until they are. Revisions are included. If a change goes beyond the original order, we tell you before we do it.
Can you handle the Christmas rush?
Yes. We have built more than 2000 models in the last 11 months and run dedicated teams for large volumes. Tell us your expected volume and we plan for it.
Which files can you deliver?
STL, 3MF, OBJ, PLY, STEP, FBX, GLB and the Blender source. 3MF can come with plates, parts and colours already set for your slicer.
Can you work with my printer?
Yes. FDM, resin, SLS, multicolour or a custom build. Tell us the build volume, nozzle or resolution and we set wall thickness and detail size to match. See technical specs.
Do you make LED lanterns and night lights?
Yes. We cut the windows through, hollow the shell for an LED, add a removable base and keep walls even so the light glows evenly.
Can you split the model for painting or multicolour?
Yes. Roofs, walls, shutters and doors can be separate parts with alignment pins and 0.2 mm clearance.
Do you offer white label 3D modeling?
Yes. We work behind your shop name. Your customers only ever deal with you, and our files carry no CubiX branding. Many shops use us as their in house modeling team.
Who owns the files?
You do, once they are paid for. Reprint, resell and edit them freely.
Will you ever contact my customers?
Never. Nothing we deliver carries our name, and we are happy to sign an NDA.
Do you show my orders in your portfolio?
Only with your written permission, and with house numbers, street names, faces and plates removed.
