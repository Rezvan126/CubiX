## Platform

web

## Users

Owners and small teams running Etsy shops (and similar storefronts: Shopify, Amazon Handmade, Instagram) that sell custom 3D printed house replicas, LED house lanterns, keepsakes and landmark models. Mostly based in the US and Europe. They own the printers and the customer relationship; modeling each custom house from buyer photos is their bottleneck, especially in seasonal peaks (Christmas, Mother's Day, housewarming season). They are busy and skim: the site must explain the offer and working model in about 20 seconds.

Secondary: shop owners who find CubiX through Upwork or Fiverr and want to check credibility before hiring there.

## Product Purpose

CubiX is a white label 3D modeling service. A shop sends the buyer's house photos (or architect drawings); CubiX returns a print ready, checked 3D file within 24 to 48 hours. The shop prints, finishes, ships and only then pays. Success means a shop sends its first free order, then keeps sending orders every week instead of closing custom listings when busy.

## Positioning

A production team, not a single freelancer: 2000+ house models completed in the last 11 months, dedicated teams for large volumes, 24 to 48 hour maximum turnaround every day including weekends and holidays, payment only after the shop's customer approves, and revisions until approved. The team handles the whole modeling job (reference review, modeling, print preparation, previews, revisions, file delivery), so the shop can print and deliver more.

## Operating Context

- Order flow: shop uploads photos and details (order form, email, or later a client portal), CubiX confirms within minutes, preview renders within 24 hours, revisions, final files within 48 hours, shop prints, then pays.
- Payment channels: Upwork, Fiverr, Payoneer, or direct (bank transfer or Wise). Upwork and Fiverr profile links are given by email on request, not published.
- Contact: email only, rizwan@coraxys.com. No WhatsApp.
- Slicers the files target: Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer, OrcaSlicer, Cura, Chitubox, Lychee.
- Seasonality matters: holiday capacity messaging is part of the offer.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Output formats: STL, 3MF (plates, parts, colour assignment), OBJ, PLY, STEP, FBX, GLB, BLEND.
- Print methods prepared for: FDM, MSLA and SLA resin, SLS and MJF, multicolour (AMS, MMU), LED lanterns, large format and custom printers.
- Print preparation: watertight manifold meshes, wall thickness per process, minimum feature size, overhang limits, hollowing with drain holes, split parts with alignment pins and clearance, chamfered base, lantern light cavities.
- Typical price $50 to $150 per model, depending on size, detail, print method and extras; fixed quote before work starts. First model free. No upfront payment.
- Site is static HTML/CSS/JS deployed on Cloudflare Pages; backend (order storage, client portal, CMS) will be built later. The client portal is shown as design screens only and is not live yet.
- Copy rule: no dashes in visible text. Plain, human, to the point. No fluff and nothing that reads as AI generated.

## Product Principles

1. Clarity in 20 seconds: what we do, how it works, what it costs, when you pay.
2. Proof over promises: show real photo to model comparisons before asking for anything.
3. Remove risk: free first model, pay after approval, revisions until approved.
4. Respect the shop's ownership: white label, files belong to the shop, never contact their customers.
5. Speak like a production partner: precise, technical where it helps, never salesy.
