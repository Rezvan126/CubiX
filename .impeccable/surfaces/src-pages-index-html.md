# CubiX marketing site

Scope: the whole CubiX marketing site (home, work, how it works, pricing, specs, about, FAQ, start, privacy). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: busy Etsy print shop owners who must grasp the offer and working model in about 20 seconds, be convinced by real photo to model proof, then send a free first order. Proof on hand: 24 real projects with customer photos (permission given), 2000+ models in 11 months, dedicated teams for large volumes. Constraints: no dashes in visible copy, email only contact, Upwork and Fiverr links by email request, Axys3D in footer only, no invented testimonials or logos. Backend comes later; forms fall back to email.

Memorable moment: drop the customer's photos straight onto the box lid and the order sheet opens with them attached.

Unresolved: client portal is mockup only; real Upwork and Fiverr links pending.

## Direction contract

THESIS: CubiX is presented as a premium European model kit: the home page is the box lid, the process is the instruction sheet, the portfolio is the parts catalogue, pricing is the catalogue price list. It refuses the split SaaS hero over a row of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Board white ground with a committed kit blue that owns whole regions (lid band, instruction headers, footer), signal yellow reserved for scale badges and the primary action, sprue grey runners framing parts with gate nubs, ink for text. Condensed heavy box lettering in caps, plain readable body, mono part numbers like CX 0007. Flat colour planes with no outlines (raise from Ikko Tanaka planes: total palette commitment). Orthogonal leader lines join callouts to parts (raise from Kraftwerk wiring). The 48 hour process is one unbroken step row with a running light marking now (raise from drum machine step row). Portfolio runs in strict numbered rows (raise from film strip banding).

STORY: Visitor understands photos in, print ready model out within 48 hours, pay after approval. Believes it through the photo to model slider, numbered parts catalogue and 2000+ figure. Does: drops photos or presses Start free order, completes the order sheet.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full width lid. Blue band across the top with the CubiX box wordmark large in condensed caps and a yellow scale badge. Below on board white: left, headline in box lettering, one line of subcopy, a working drop zone framed as a sprue with the yellow Start free order action; right, the photo to model slider as box art with leader line callouts. Along the lid bottom, a CONTENTS side panel strip listing what every order includes plus 2000+ models.
