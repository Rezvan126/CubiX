Privacy and confidentiality

Last updated November 2026

What we collect

When you send an order or email us, we keep your name, email, shop link, the order details and the files you upload. We only receive information from you, the shop. We never collect anything directly from your customers.

How we use it

To build and deliver your models, reply to you, send invoices and handle reorders. We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use your customers' photos for anything else.

Your customers' photos

Photos and models are confidential. We show a project only with the shop's written permission, after removing house numbers, street names, faces and number plates. Source photos are deleted 90 days after delivery unless you ask us to keep them for reorders.

Where files are stored

In private cloud storage, open only to the team working on your order.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete anything we hold about you or your orders. Email rizwan@coraxys.com.
