The working model

Six steps, one owner

You stay in charge of your shop and your customer. We take the slow, technical part from the moment the references arrive until the file is on your printer.

Get a free model
  1. 1

    Send the order

    Upload your customer's photos, sketch or drawing on the order page, or simply email them. Add the size, your printer and the files you need, or leave it to us to ask.

    You, 2 min
  2. 2

    We confirm the brief

    We check every reference, ask about anything unclear and confirm your delivery time. If part of the object is hidden, we tell you how we will handle it.

    Within minutes
  3. 3

    Modeling and print prep

    A modeler builds it to scale, then prepares it for your printer: wall thickness, overhangs, split parts and mesh checks.

    Same day
  4. 4

    Preview for your customer

    Clean preview renders to forward. Your customer asks for changes, we make them, as many rounds as it takes.

    Within 24 h
  5. 5

    Final files delivered

    Checked, scaled and ready to slice. 3MF files can arrive with plates and colours already set.

    Within 48 h
  6. 6

    Print, then pay

    Print it and check it. Then pay through Upwork, Fiverr, Payoneer or directly. Regular shops can pay once a month.

    After approval

What we need from you

Good references make fast models

Send whatever your customer has. These help us deliver faster and closer to the real thing.

  • A clear front photo or a sketch with measurements.
  • Extra angles if your customer has them. Street View and listing photos work for buildings.
  • The finished size, for example 6 inches or 150 mm wide.
  • Your printer and material, so details are thick enough to print.
  • Special requests such as pets, cars, a name plaque or seasonal details.
  • Drawings or floor plans if you have them. We build from elevations too.
