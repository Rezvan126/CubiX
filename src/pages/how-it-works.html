What we need from you

Good references make fast models

Send whatever your customer has. These help us deliver faster and closer to the real thing.

  • A clear front photo or a sketch with measurements.
  • Extra angles if your customer has them. Street View and listing photos work for buildings.
  • The finished size, for example 6 inches or 150 mm wide.
  • Your printer and material, so details are thick enough to print.
  • Special requests such as pets, cars, a name plaque or seasonal details.
  • Drawings or floor plans if you have them. We build from elevations too.
